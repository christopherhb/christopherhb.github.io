Hashtag Generator Privacy Policy

Version 3.0.0 · Effective 10 October 2026

This policy describes Hashtag Generator version 3.0.0, provided by Christopher Bong (34WERWE). It does not describe earlier versions that included advertising and analytics.

Information handled by the app

The app generates hashtag suggestions on your device. It does not send your searches, saved collections, device identifiers or usage data to the developer. This version has no advertising, analytics, accounts or network requests.

Saved collections

Collections are stored in the app's local storage. You can delete them in the Saved tab or clear the app's storage in Android settings. Android cloud backup is disabled for this app. Device-to-device transfer behavior may depend on your device manufacturer.

Clipboard

When you tap Copy, selected hashtags are written to the system clipboard. Other apps may access that clipboard according to your operating system's rules. The app does not read your existing clipboard.

Children

This version does not collect personal information from any user and has no messaging, public profiles or content-sharing service.

Contact

For questions about this app or its privacy practices, email 34werweapps@gmail.com. If you email us, the information you choose to include is handled through our email provider to respond to your request.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes are introduced.
